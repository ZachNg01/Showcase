How to play

  • Click a tile only when it is bright and uncovered.
  • Three matching emojis disappear from your rack automatically.
  • You lose if your rack reaches seven tiles before a merge.
  • Use shuffle once, and use hint to highlight the best currently playable tile or match.
Latest fix: the game board now centres the actual tile stack using measured tile bounds, and hint highlights the best currently playable move.
